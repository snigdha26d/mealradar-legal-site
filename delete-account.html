Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your MealRadar account and data at any time. You do not need to reinstall the app to request deletion.

Delete your account inside the app

  1. Open MealRadar and go to Home.
  2. Open the Account drawer (the account icon).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the two-step confirmation prompt.

Delete Account is available when you are signed in to a registered MealRadar account. Guest/anonymous mode does not use a registered MealRadar account, so this registered-account deletion control is not shown.

What deletion does

  • Permanently deletes your MealRadar account from our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Clears MealRadar app data stored on that device — including pantry, saved recipes, saved meal plans, shopping list, shopping habits, and preferences — after the account is removed.
  • Returns the app to a guest state.

What may remain

Because most content is stored only on the device, deletion clears the data on the device you used. Data on a different device you separately signed in on would need to be cleared on that device too. We delete or de-identify personal data we control when it is no longer needed, when consent is withdrawn, or when deletion is requested, unless retention is needed for security, billing, legal compliance, dispute handling, fraud or abuse prevention, or another lawful reason; such records are not used to restore your deleted content.

Your device's operating system or platform provider may also hold its own backup of locally stored app data, and may restore it if you reinstall the app. MealRadar cannot delete or control those platform backups — manage them through your device or platform account backup settings.

To prevent abuse of our free AI credits, MealRadar may keep a minimal pseudonymous anti-abuse record after your account is deleted. This record contains only a server-side hashed (HMAC) form of your email address and a count of free AI generations used. It does not include your raw email address, pantry items, recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, prompts, or account profile data, and it is used only to enforce free-credit limits and prevent fraud — not to identify you or restore deleted content.

Imported recipes. Recipes you import and save are stored on your device like your other saved recipes; MealRadar does not sync them to cloud storage. Deleting a saved recipe, or deleting your account, removes saved recipes from the app but does not delete preview image files saved on your device during import. Those files may remain in the app's storage until the app is uninstalled.

Separately from the anti-abuse record above, limited operational records created when you used recipe importing — such as how many import requests you made — may remain after your account is deleted, linked to your former account identifier. They do not include the recipe, captions, or transcripts. These records currently have no automatic expiry or scheduled deletion. See the Privacy Policy for details.

If you chose to submit a report about an AI result, that report may also remain after your account is deleted, linked to your former account identifier. It may include the reason you selected and any optional comment you added.

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google Play

Deleting your MealRadar account does not automatically cancel an active subscription. Subscriptions are billed and managed by the app store you purchased through, not by MealRadar.

  • If you subscribed through the App Store, manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple / App Store account settings.
  • If you subscribed through Google Play, manage or cancel the subscription in your Google Play account subscription settings.

Apple, Google Play, and RevenueCat may retain transaction records needed for billing, tax, fraud prevention, refund handling, and legal compliance. We cannot delete those provider records.

Request deletion by email

If you are unable to open the app or complete in-app deletion — or you simply prefer to ask us — you can request account and data deletion or erasure by emailing support@mealradar.xyz from the email associated with your account. We will verify your request and process the deletion of data we control. Users in India may also use this address for data-rights and grievance requests; we aim to respond within 90 days unless a shorter period is required by applicable law.
