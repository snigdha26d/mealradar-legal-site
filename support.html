Support

Need help with MealRadar? This page covers common questions and how to reach us.

Getting help & reporting a problem

If something isn't working, please email us with:

  • your device and operating system (for example, iPhone / iOS or Android),
  • the app version if you know it,
  • what you were doing and what happened.

Contact: support@mealradar.xyz.

Account help

You can use MealRadar as a guest, or create and sign into an account from the Home account drawer. From there you can also sign out or delete your account. For step-by-step deletion instructions, see the Account & Data Deletion page.

About your data (important)

Your pantry, saved recipes, saved meal plans, shopping list, shopping habits, and preferences are primarily stored locally on your device. MealRadar does not provide its own cloud sync or cloud backup service for this data, and cross-device sync is not currently available. If you uninstall the app, clear its storage, or lose or replace your device, this data may be lost. Your device's operating system or platform provider may separately back up and later restore locally stored app data depending on your device and platform account backup settings; those backups are outside MealRadar's control. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Data-rights & grievance requests

For privacy and data-rights requests — including access, correction, updating, deletion or erasure, withdrawal of consent, grievances, or (in India) nomination-related requests — email support@mealradar.xyz from the email associated with your account. We aim to respond within 90 days unless a shorter period is required by applicable law. See the Privacy Policy for details on what we process, our service providers, retention, and breach handling.

Recipe importing FAQ

How do I import a recipe? On iPhone, open a recipe post or web page, tap Share, and choose MealRadar from the Share Sheet (you may need to tap More to find it). For recipe websites, you can also paste the link into the app using Paste Recipe Link in Recipes.

Which sources are supported? You can share recipes from Instagram, Facebook Reels, YouTube, Pinterest, and recipe websites (for example, from Safari). Pasting a link in the app works for recipe websites; for social-media posts, use Share instead.

Is there a video length limit? Only for audio transcription. When a recipe is spoken in a video rather than written in the caption, MealRadar can currently transcribe the audio for eligible social-media videos up to 2 minutes long (120 seconds). This limit applies only to audio transcription. It does not limit which videos you can play or which recipes you can import; for longer videos, MealRadar uses the caption and other available details instead.

Why is my imported recipe incomplete? Not every creator includes full ingredients or instructions in their post, caption, or video. MealRadar can only use what is available, so you may need to review the recipe and fill in missing details yourself before saving.

What if an import fails? If you are offline or recipe import is temporarily unavailable, MealRadar shows a Retry button so you can try again. If no recipe details could be found, MealRadar lets you enter or paste the recipe yourself instead. If a share contains nothing MealRadar can import, try sharing the recipe link or recipe text instead.

How do videos play? MealRadar does not host videos. Supported videos play through their original platform, so a video may stop playing if it is removed, made private, or restricted by its creator or platform.

Should I check imported recipes? Yes. Recipe details are extracted automatically and may be incomplete or inaccurate. Always review ingredients, quantities, instructions, allergens, and food-safety information before cooking. See the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy for more about recipe importing.

Food safety & allergy disclaimer

MealRadar can suggest recipes and meal plans, and includes basic allergy and expired-food safety guards. These are conveniences, not guarantees. MealRadar does not provide medical, nutritional, dietary, or allergy advice. Always check ingredients, expiry dates, and allergy suitability yourself, and follow safe food-handling practices.
